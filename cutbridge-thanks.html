Thank you. Your key is on its way.

Check your inbox for an e-mail from CutBridge <contact@cutatlas.io> with your licence key. Paddle sends the receipt separately.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the latest CutBridge .pkg and double-click it. macOS will ask for your password.
  2. Open Premiere Pro. The panel is under Window → UXP Plugins → CutBridge. If it is not there yet, log out and back in once — the panel registers on login.
  3. Accept the licence terms in the panel, paste your key, and release your first package.

Requirements

Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 11 or newer) and Premiere Pro 26.3 or newer. One licence is active on one Mac at a time; use Remove from this Mac in the panel before moving.

No e-mail after 10 minutes?

Check spam, then write to contact@cutatlas.io from the address you used at checkout — we will resend the key by hand.
