Finishing-ready turnover

When a cut is locked, the project still isn't ready for anyone downstream. Color, online, and sound each need it rebuilt their way: the right sequences, the right exports, the right names, the right folders. So someone sits down and does it by hand: duplicating timelines, stripping effects, renaming, exporting FCP XML and AAF, rendering reference videos.

It is repetitive, easy to get wrong, and the mistakes surface downstream, after the project has already left editorial. CutBridge does this preparation in a structured, repeatable way instead.

03 What sets it apart

Built for the conform side, not just the export.

A converter rebuilds a timeline for one application. A turnover is more than that, and it should never require uploading your project to get it.

LOCAL ONLY

Nothing leaves your Mac

CutBridge runs entirely on your machine. Your projects, media and deliverables never leave it: nothing is uploaded to be converted, no project file sits on anyone's server. The only things that ever go out are your licence details and, if you opt in, an anonymised error report. Under an NDA or a client security mandate, that is the whole conversation. And if the connection drops mid-job, CutBridge keeps working for seven days without one.

FULL PACKAGE

The whole turnover, released at once

Not one file but the whole package: grade and online sequences, FCP XML, AAF, burn-in references, the sound prep with its marker list, a folder layout the conform suite expects and a manifest of what is inside. Released together and validated before anything leaves editorial.

COLOUR-CODED

References that show where every frame came from

Burned onto the render that comes out of Premiere, so the reference is the edit by definition. Every visible layer gets its own colour: an outline on the frame, the full source file name, a running source timecode, the speed when a shot is retimed, and record timecode along the bottom. Stacks, picture-in-picture, nests and speed ramps included.

FAQ

Questions about CutBridge.

What is CutBridge?

CutBridge is an Adobe Premiere Pro plugin that automates the handoff of a finished edit to post-production departments: color grading, online/conform, and audio post.

Who is it for?

It is built for senior freelance editors and assistant editors, small post-production houses, and online/conform specialists, people who need reliable, finishing-ready exports.

How do I get started?

Start a 7-day free trial from the pricing section above. No card, and the key arrives by e-mail within a minute. When you're ready, subscribe annually or monthly; your trial key simply stops and the paid key takes over.

Which platforms does it support?

Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 11 or newer) with Adobe Premiere Pro 26.3 or newer. Windows and Intel Macs are planned. Leave your e-mail below and we will tell you when they ship.

What about data privacy?

Your projects, media and deliverables never leave your Mac. Nothing is uploaded to be converted or checked. The only things that go out are your licence details and, if you opt in, an anonymised error report. Every field is listed in the CutBridge Privacy Policy.

Why not just hand the .prproj to the colorist and let a converter deal with it?

Two reasons. A converter rebuilds the timeline for one application; it does not produce the burn-in references, the sound prep, the folder structure, the manifest or the check that everything was actually created. That package is what downstream teams expect. And CutBridge runs entirely on your Mac: nothing is uploaded to be converted, and no project file sits on anyone's server. What leaves editorial is what you chose to release, and nothing else.

What if a package comes out wrong?

Write to contact@cutatlas.io with the project and what the colorist or sound team rejected. We reproduce it and ship a fix within 14 days, or, within 30 days of purchase, refund you in full. Every format fix we ship starts with somebody's real project, so we would rather have the report.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to activate the licence. After that CutBridge checks in when it can; if the network drops, on location, in a hotel or in a suite that keeps editorial machines off the internet, it keeps working for seven days before it needs to reach us again. Your project never goes online either way.

How much does CutBridge cost?

$149 per year at the founding price (regular $179), or $29 per month if you only need it for a project. Both start with a 7-day free trial with no card, and both come with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

What does "founding price" mean?

The first 100 annual subscribers, or anyone who subscribes before 31 December 2026, pay $149 a year for as long as their subscription stays active. If you cancel and come back later, the regular price applies.

What happens if I cancel?

CutBridge keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for, then stops releasing packages. Every package you have already produced stays yours, in open formats (XML, AAF, MP4, CSV) that need neither us nor the plugin.

Can I use one licence on two Macs?

One licence is active on one Mac at a time. Moving is normal: click "Remove from this Mac" in the panel and activate on the other one.

Windows or Intel Mac?

Not on Apple Silicon yet?

CutBridge ships for Apple Silicon first. Leave your e-mail and we will write when Windows and Intel builds are ready, and nothing else.
